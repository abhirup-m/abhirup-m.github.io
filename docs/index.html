Hey There!

Welcome to my website. I am Abhirup, a research scholar at the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research Kolkata (IISER Kolkata), in India. I work in theoretical condensed matter physics, and am a part of the Emergent Phenomena and Quantum Matter (EPQM) group. I study various topics in the field of strongly correlated materials. I also happen to be the co-founder of IISER Kolkata’s science magazine InScight, and currently lead the graphic design and web-development unit.
